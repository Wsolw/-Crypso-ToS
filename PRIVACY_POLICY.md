# Privacy Policy for Crypso

**Last Updated:** May 2026

Crypso ("Bot", "we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the Crypso Discord bot and how it is used.

## 1. Information We Collect

When using Crypso, we may collect and store:

### Discord Information
- Discord User ID
- Username / Display Name
- Server ID
- Server Invite Link
- Channel ID (for command processing)
- Message interactions related to bot commands

### Wallet & Transaction Information
- Internal wallet balances
- Deposit addresses assigned to users
- Withdrawal addresses entered by users
- Tip transaction history
- Deposit / withdrawal timestamps
- Blockchain transaction hashes (TXIDs)

### Technical Information
- Error logs
- Usage logs
- Security monitoring logs

## 2. How We Use Information

We use collected data to:

- Operate tipping, deposit, and withdrawal systems
- Process wallet balances and transactions
- Prevent fraud, abuse, spam, or unauthorized access
- Improve bot stability and performance
- Provide support when needed
- Comply with legal obligations if required

## 3. Blockchain Transparency Notice

Cryptocurrency transactions are recorded on public blockchains. Deposits, withdrawals, and transfers may be publicly viewable depending on the blockchain used.

## 4. Data Sharing

We do **not** sell user data.

We may share limited data only when necessary with:

- Blockchain service providers
- Hosting / infrastructure providers
- Security / anti-abuse systems
- Law enforcement when legally required

## 5. Data Security

We use reasonable security measures to protect stored data. However, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

Users are responsible for protecting their Discord account and device security.

## 6. Financial Risk Disclaimer

Cryptocurrency involves market risk, volatility, network delays, and possible loss of funds. Users are responsible for actions taken using Crypso.

Crypso does not provide financial advice.

## 7. Withdrawals & User Responsibility

Users are responsible for entering correct withdrawal addresses. Transactions sent to incorrect or unsupported addresses may be irreversible.

## 8. Data Retention

We may retain account, wallet, and transaction data as long as necessary for operations, security, dispute resolution, and legal compliance.

## 9. Children’s Privacy

Crypso is not intended for users under the minimum age required by Discord or local laws.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Continued use of Crypso means acceptance of updated terms.

## 11. Contact

For support or privacy questions, contact the Crypso support team through official Discord channels.
